About ZETA Association

ZETA Association (Zone for Electronics Technologies and Automation) is a student-driven technical organization established under the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at BEHARA COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY.

The association encourages innovation, technical excellence, leadership, teamwork, industrial exposure, and project development among engineering students.

Objectives

  • Promote technical innovation.
  • Conduct workshops and seminars.
  • Encourage project development.
  • Develop leadership qualities.
  • Create industry-ready engineers.

Why ZETA?

ZETA provides students with opportunities to improve technical knowledge, communication skills, leadership qualities, industrial exposure, project development, and professional networking.
